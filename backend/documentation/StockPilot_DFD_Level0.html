Reading the diagram

  • External entities (Investor, Financial Analyst, Administrator, Yahoo Finance API) sit outside the system and exchange data with it.
  • Processes 1.0–6.0 are the platform's main functions; each can be decomposed further in a Level 1 DFD (e.g. 2.0 → factor scoring, verdict, sentiment).
  • Data stores D1–D7 group the PostgreSQL tables. A plain line (—) between a process and a store denotes read/write access; arrows show directional data flows.
  • Yahoo Finance supplies live prices, historical OHLCV, and news headlines used by the AI analysis and backtesting.
